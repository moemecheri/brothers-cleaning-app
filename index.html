Where these prices come from
  • Airbnb turnovers in Ottawa: studios about $70–$110, 1-bedrooms $80–$140, 2-bedrooms $110–$180, 3-bedroom houses $150–$260, 4-bedrooms $220–$380. Linen service adds $15–$40 and last-minute bookings 15–25% (Genie 2026 Ottawa guide, CleanFix Pro, Ottawa Cleaners, checked Oct 2026). The intro price sits just under the cheapest of these.
  • Small Ottawa offices usually pay about $0.15–$0.25 per sq ft per visit, so roughly $100–$250 for 1,000 sq ft (Hellamaid, Ottawa office rates).
  • Across Canada, recurring office cleaning runs $0.10–$0.25 per sq ft per visit, or $30–$60 an hour per cleaner. Bigger offices pay less per sq ft (HomeStars 2026). The app lowers the rate as size goes up, and per visit for offices cleaned several times a week.
  • A standard 3-hour house clean in Ottawa is about $135–$180 (Hellamaid, Ottawa house rates). Deep cleans run about $270–$410 and move-outs $260–$450 in Canada (HomeStars).
  • Recurring house discounts of 10% monthly, 15% every 2 weeks and 20% weekly match what big Ottawa companies advertise.
  • Every price is kept at $35 or more per work hour, so you still cover gas, supplies and your time. New teams are often slower at first, so time yourselves on the first few jobs.

How to get office leads this week
  1. Walk in to 15–20 offices a day

    Start with plazas and office buildings in Orléans and the east end. Close to home means less driving per job. Go 10–11:30 am or 2–4 pm, ask for the office manager, leave a flyer, and offer a free walkthrough with a quote the next day.

  2. Call commercial property managers

    One manager can hand you several buildings. Ask who handles cleaning contracts and how to get on their vendor list. They'll ask for insurance, so get it first.

  3. Go after small clinics, dentists and daycares

    They need cleaning every week and big companies often skip small sites. Clinics pay more because the work needs more care.

  4. Answer every online lead within 5 minutes

    Bark, HomeStars, Kijiji, Facebook Marketplace and local Facebook groups. The first cleaner to reply often wins.

  5. Set up Google Business Profile and get 5 reviews

    Do a few house cleans for friends and family at your starter price, then ask each one for a Google review.

  6. Follow up twice

    Message 3 days after a quote, then again at 10 days. Many jobs are won on the follow-up, not the first visit. Put each follow-up as the next step on the lead.

  7. Offer a referral thank-you

    For example, $50 off the next month for any client who sends you a business that signs.
